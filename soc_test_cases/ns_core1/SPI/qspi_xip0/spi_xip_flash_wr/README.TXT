Function description:
    This demo is used to test SPI_XIP erase/write/read flash model in xip mode.

Test result:
    the test results are printed out via USART PASS/FAIL/TODO.

IO Connection information:
    Connect QSPI_XIP0SCK to Flash SCK .
    Connect QSPI_XIP0CS  to Flash CS  .
    Connect QSPI_XIP0DQ0 to Flash DQ0 .
    Connect QSPI_XIP0DQ1 to Flash DQ1 .
    Connect QSPI_XIP0DQ2 to Flash DQ2 .
    Connect QSPI_XIP0DQ3 to Flash DQ3 .
