Function description:
    This demo is used to test SPI transport data in spi 4 line mode.

Test result:
    the test results are printed out via USART PASS/FAIL/TODO.

IO Connection information:
    Connect QSPI1SCK    to QSPI2SCK  .
    Connect QSPI1CS     to QSPI2CS  .
    Connect QSPI1DQ0    to QSPI2DQ0 .
    Connect QSPI1DQ1    to QSPI2DQ1 .
    Connect QSPI1DQ2    to QSPI2DQ2 .
    Connect QSPI1DQ3    to QSPI2DQ3 .
